# Customer Churn Analysis

## Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze customer churn behavior and identify the key factors influencing customer retention and customer loss. The project includes both Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and Data Visualization to uncover meaningful insights from customer data.

## Dataset
**IBM Telco Customer Churn Dataset**

The dataset contains customer demographics, service details, billing information, contract types, satisfaction scores, and churn-related attributes used to analyze customer behavior and retention.

## Key Findings
- The overall customer churn rate is **26.54%**.
- Month-to-Month contracts show the highest churn levels.
- Fiber Optic customers exhibit higher churn compared to other internet service users.
- Customers with lower satisfaction scores are more likely to churn.
- Competitor-related factors are the leading causes of customer churn.
- Better competitor offers and devices are the most common churn reasons.

## Conclusion
Customer churn is influenced by multiple factors including contract type, customer satisfaction, internet service type, billing preferences, and competitor offerings. The insights derived from this project can help organizations design effective retention strategies and improve customer loyalty.
